TASK

Find three facts about one company: its legal form, its registered address and its authorized legal representatives. The company is named in the COMPANY section at the end of this prompt. Research it on the web now, following every rule below, and answer in the exact format of the OUTPUT section. Do not ask questions. Do not explain. Do not answer from memory: use only what you find during this research. If you cannot search the web, answer with the three standard texts of section 6.


1. THE EXACT ENTITY

1.1 The company is one legal entity, identified by its full legal name including its legal form, for example "Foo S.A. de C.V." or "Foo GmbH".
1.2 A place written after a comma in the name ("Foo S.A., Quito") or a code before the name ("D10629 Foo S.A.") tells you where the company is or how the team files it. It is not part of the legal name.
1.3 The entity you report on must have the same legal name and the same legal form as the COMPANY, in the COUNTRY when one is given. Differences in capital letters, accents, punctuation and spacing do not matter.
1.4 Never use information about a parent company, subsidiary, branch, affiliate, shareholder, group company, brand, franchise, former name or any other entity with a similar name. Each one is a different entity.
1.5 If two or more entities match and you cannot tell which one is meant, treat the company as not found: use the standard texts of section 6 for the address and the representatives, and take the legal form from the name as section 3.3 says.


2. WHERE TO LOOK, IN THIS ORDER OF TRUST

Tier 1, official registers and authorities of the company's country:
commercial registers, company registers, tax authority lookups, official gazettes and supervisory authorities.
Tier 2, the company's own legal notices:
the legal notice of its website (Impressum, Aviso legal, Aviso de privacidad, Política de tratamiento de datos, Términos y condiciones, Mentions légales), its official filings, invoices and letterhead.
Tier 3, the Global LEI Index at search.gleif.org, which gives the legal name, legal form and registered address of entities that have an LEI.
Tier 4, business information sites that copy official registers, for example North Data, OpenCorporates, Moneyhouse, Dun & Bradstreet and Kompass.
Tier 5, news, LinkedIn, social media and directories.

2.1 Search in the company's own language as well as in English. Use the local words for the facts you need, for example "Geschäftsführer", "Sitz", "Handelsregister", "representante legal", "gerente general", "domicilio fiscal", "administrador único", "sócio administrador".
2.2 Tier 1 and Tier 2 confirm a fact. Tier 3 confirms the legal name, legal form and address only.
2.3 Tier 4 and Tier 5 only give leads. Look for a Tier 1 or Tier 2 source that confirms the lead. A fact found only in Tier 4 or Tier 5 must carry the caution of section 5.
2.4 When sources disagree, the higher tier wins. Within the same tier, the source with the most recent date wins.
2.5 Companies with little online presence are often found only in official registers, official gazettes and the legal notice of their own website. Check these before you conclude that nothing exists.

3. LEGAL FORM

3.1 Write the short legal form as the company's legal name writes it, with its conventional capital letters and periods, for example "GmbH", "GmbH & Co. KG", "AG", "S.A.", "S.A. de C.V.", "S. de R.L. de C.V.", "S.A.S.", "Ltda.", "C.A.", "LLC", "Ltd", "SpA", "S.R.L.", "EIRL".
3.2 Never write the long form ("Sociedad Anónima", "Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung") and never translate it.
3.3 If the legal name contains the legal form, use it. If it does not, use the legal form that a Tier 1 or Tier 2 source states.


4. ADDRESS AND REPRESENTATIVES

4.1 Address: the registered office of the entity (Sitz, domicilio social, domicilio fiscal, sede). Use the head office only when no registered office is published. Never use a branch, a warehouse or a sales office.
4.2 Write the address on one line in this order: street and number, postal code, city, state or region when the country uses one, country. Separate the parts with commas. Write the street and the city as the source writes them, with their accents. Write the country in English.
4.3 Write only the parts that a source states. Never guess a street, a number or a postal code. An address with missing parts is allowed and must carry the caution of section 5.
4.4 Authorized representatives are the natural persons who may legally act for and sign for the entity: managing directors, legal representatives, general managers, sole administrators, members of a board who represent the company, holders of a general power of attorney or Prokura.
4.5 Do not list shareholders, partners, auditors, statutory examiners (comisarios), supervisory board members, founders or employees unless a source shows that they hold one of the roles in 4.4.
4.6 When another company is the administrator or general partner (for example the GmbH of a GmbH & Co. KG), do not list that company. List the natural persons who represent it, only if a Tier 1 source names them, with their role followed by "of the General Partner" or "of the Corporate Administrator", for example: Hans Müller (Managing Director of the General Partner)
4.7 List only persons whose role is current. Leave out every person whose appointment ended, who resigned or who died.
4.8 Write each person as: Full Name (Role). A person with several roles: Full Name (Role 1, Role 2). Separate persons with a comma and a space. Example: Ana María Pérez Gómez (Legal Representative, General Manager), Hans Müller (Managing Director)
4.9 Write the full name exactly as the best source writes it, with all given names, surnames and accents. Never abbreviate a name. Never add titles such as Dr., Ing., Lic. or Mag.
4.10 Write each role in English. Use these translations:
Geschäftsführer, Geschäftsführerin, gérant: Managing Director
Gerente General: General Manager
Gerente, Socio Gerente: Manager
Representante Legal: Legal Representative
Representante Legal Suplente: Alternate Legal Representative
Administrador Único: Sole Administrator
Vorstand, Vorstandsmitglied: Member of the Management Board
Vorstandsvorsitzender, Vorstandsvorsitzende: Chair of the Management Board
Presidente del Directorio, Presidente del Consejo de Administración: Chair of the Board of Directors
Director, Directora, Consejero, Consejera: Member of the Board of Directors
Prokurist, Prokuristin: Authorized Signatory (Prokura)
Apoderado, Apoderado General, Procurador: Attorney-in-Fact
Sócio Administrador, Socio Administrador: Managing Partner
Presidente, Secretario, Tesorero (officers of a board): President, Secretary, Treasurer
For any other role, use the plain English job title that has the same meaning.


5. THE CAUTION

5.1 A field gets the caution in each of these cases:
a) no Tier 1 or Tier 2 source confirms its value,
b) the only confirming source is a dated publication (a gazette entry, a filing, annual accounts) more than three years old, and no current register lookup or current company website confirms it,
c) sources disagree and you chose by the rule of 2.4,
d) the address has missing parts.
A current register lookup or print and the company's current website are never too old.
5.2 The caution is this exact sentence, added at the end of the field's line after a period and one space. If the value already ends with a period, add only the space.
Caution: This field might differ from internal sources or be inaccurate due to insufficient information online
5.3 Use the caution only for a value you found. Never use it instead of the standard texts of section 6, and never use a standard text when you found a plausible value.


6. THE STANDARD TEXTS WHEN NOTHING IS FOUND

Use these texts exactly, character for character, when no value can be found in any source after the research of section 2:

7. WRITING RULES

7.1 Write plain text only. No Markdown, no bold, no italics, no headings, no bullets, no numbering, no tables, no code blocks, no emojis.
7.2 Never use an em dash, an en dash, a semicolon, an ellipsis, curly quotation marks, footnote marks or reference numbers, even where grammar would allow them. Separate parts with commas and end sentences with periods. Hyphens, apostrophes, "&" and other characters that are part of a name, an address or a legal form stay as the source writes them.
7.3 Write each field on a single line. Never break a field over several lines.
7.4 Do not add sources, links, citations, notes, comments, greetings, an introduction or a conclusion.
7.5 Keep the letters with accents and other characters of names and addresses as the source writes them.
